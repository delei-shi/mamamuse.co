GOOD TO KNOW

常见问题 · FAQ

零基础可以参加吗？ Can complete beginners join?

完全可以，我们大部分学员都是从零开始的。每个动作都会拆解到位，用「刻意练习」的方法反复带练，节奏温和又不失活力，一节课下来你会发现身体比想象中听话。Absolutely — most of our dancers started from zero. Every move is broken down step by step and drilled with deliberate practice, so beginners build real technique without feeling rushed.

需要穿什么、带什么？ What should I wear and bring?

舒适、方便活动的衣物，一双干净的室内运动鞋，再带上水就可以了 —— 不需要任何舞蹈基础或专业装备。Comfortable activewear, clean indoor sneakers, and a water bottle — no dance background or special gear needed.

如何预约试课？ How do I book a trial class?

添加微信 shidelei002，或发邮件至 d@mamamuse.co，备注「姓名 + 试听 + 舞蹈/运动经历」，老师会尽快回复为你安排。首次课程完全免费。Add us on WeChat (shidelei002) or email d@mamamuse.co with your name, "trial", and any dance or fitness background. Your first class is completely free.

怎么付款？ How do I pay?

免费试课之后再决定就好。支持 Zelle、Venmo 或现金，上课时与老师确认即可。Decide after your free trial — we accept Zelle, Venmo, or cash, just settle up with Ella in class.
